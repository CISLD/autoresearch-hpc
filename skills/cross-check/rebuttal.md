# Task: judge a rebuttal of an UNSOUND verdict

A review of this iteration returned `UNSOUND`. The producing agent has rebutted it
and wants the iteration to conclude as it stands. You decide whether the rebuttal
answers the reasons the review gave. The evidence is the same evidence the review
saw: the pre-declaration and report below have not changed since.

Judge the rebuttal, not the producer and not the iteration afresh:
1. Which of the review's reasons for `UNSOUND` does the rebuttal address?
2. For each, does it show from the evidence that the reason does not hold, or
   does it only assert, restate the plan, or promise later work?
3. Does any reason for `UNSOUND` stand unanswered?

Answer `WITHDRAWN` only if every reason for `UNSOUND` is shown not to hold for
this evidence. A rebuttal that concedes a defect but calls it minor withdraws
nothing unless it shows the defect cannot change the reported result. Answer
`MAINTAINED` otherwise. A maintained `UNSOUND` leaves the iteration unconcluded;
the correction then goes into a new iteration, so maintaining a verdict costs
the work nothing that was not already owed.

Do not raise new objections here, do not poll or invoke another model, and do
not treat the author's prediction as evidence. Return one bounded answer.
